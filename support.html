Arrangements Log

If something isn't working, or you have a question, please get in touch: md@neutrade.co.uk

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you are signed in. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. The app has no servers.

Do you get crash reports?

Only through Apple, and only if you have Apple’s “Share With App Developers” setting turned on. A crash report says the reason for the crash so it can be fixed. It contains no part of your log — no notes, no names, no dates, nothing you have typed. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can still export everything you have logged, as JSON or CSV, from the subscription screen. Your record is never deleted.

How do I get my data out?

More → Export JSON (a full backup) or Export CSV (opens in a spreadsheet). The app also keeps automatic backups you can restore from the same menu.

I deleted an entry by mistake

Automatic backups keep the previous state and a monthly archive. Use More → Import JSON and choose one — duplicates are skipped.

Will this work if I move to a new iPhone?

Yes, provided iCloud sync is on. The app tells you on its first screen whether sync is active.
